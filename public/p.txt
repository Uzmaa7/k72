Why video disappeared suddenly?

The Vimeo link you used had a time-based security token
That token expired after some time

When it expired:
The <video> tag was still rendered
But the browser was not allowed to play the video
So the background became black, and only text was visible
This is why it felt like the video “suddenly” vanished even though you didn’t change your code.

---Solution---------
stopped using the video URL
downloaded the video file

This removed:
token expiry
network permission issues
third-party dependency

placed the video inside the public folder

Problem

A single Video component was being reused for two different purposes:
as a full-screen background video
as a small inline video inside the hero text
Because the same component handled both cases, styles like rounded-full and overflow-hidden caused conflicts.
As a result, the background video was also getting rounded, and the inline video behavior could not be controlled independently.

Solution

To fix this, the video functionality was separated into two components:
BackgroundVideo → used only for the full-screen background (no rounding)
InlineVideo → used only inside the text and styled with rounded-full and overflow-hidden
This separation removed the styling conflict and ensured the inline video renders as rounded while keeping the background video normal.
